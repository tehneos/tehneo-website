For companies running AI in Croatian

Croatian AI chatbot quality audit

Your chatbot or AI assistant talks to customers in Croatian every day. Do you know how often it gets the facts wrong, sounds unnatural or gives risky advice? Our native-speaker team reviews a sample of real (anonymised) conversations and gives you a clear report with numbers and concrete fixes.

Request an audit

01

One-off audit

We review 500–1,000 conversations and flag factual errors, wrong or unnatural Croatian, unhelpful answers and unsafe advice. You get a report with error rates by category and examples.

02

Ongoing human review

Every week we review a sample of conversations plus the cases where your AI was uncertain, and send back corrections you can use to improve the model.

03

Data protection

EU-based, data processing agreement (GDPR) and NDA for every engagement. We work only with anonymised data inside your tools.
